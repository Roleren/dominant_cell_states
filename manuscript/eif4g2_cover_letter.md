Dear Editor,

We are preparing the manuscript “A tRNA-selective switch in an immunogenic
EIF4G2 upstream ORF” for consideration as an Article in *Nature*.

The work began as a systematic search for condition-dependent redistribution
of ribosome occupancy between upstream open reading frames and downstream
coding sequences in public human ribosome-profiling data. The search nominated
an unusually discrete event in EIF4G2, which encodes the non-canonically
initiated translation factor DAP5. Overexpression of tRNA-Glu(UUC), but not a
matched tRNA-Arg(CCG) perturbation that shares the source study, cell background
and metastatic phenotype, redistributes ribosome occupancy into a 51-nt ATG
uORF. This exact uORF has independently been shown to yield an HLA-presented
peptide.

We believe the observation is of broad interest because it joins three fields
that have largely remained separate: regulated tRNA pools, uORF-based routing
at a stress-responsive translation factor, and non-canonical antigen
presentation. The effect is not a sparse leader signal. It is supported by
hundreds to thousands of feature counts, separated biological-replicate ranges,
reading phase, a matched tRNA negative control, stable total RNA and isoform
composition, and coordinate and worst-position-deletion analyses. EIF4G2 is the
top tRNA-Glu-specific response among strict count-supported, non-overlapping
leader uORFs shared with the matched tRNA-Arg experiment. Reprocessing all 16
raw tRNA libraries with run- and length-specific calibration reproduced the
shift in every matched technical lane, in study-shared and canonical footprint
sets, and in the exact 51-nt uORF1. The lane-matched effect was −0.136 for
tRNA-Glu and +0.012 for tRNA-Arg, and it survived coordinate and worst-case
one- and three-position deletion challenges.

The independent recurrence is unusually stringent. We reprocessed four raw UV
Ribo-seq libraries with footprint length retained and P-site offsets calibrated
separately for every run and length. The EIF4G2 redistribution is nearly
identical in canonical 28–32-nt footprints and remains strong in short
20–23-nt footprints, with replicate-range, coordinate and greedy
position-deletion robustness in both. ABCE1 loss provides a second
recycling-related recurrence.

We have also retained results that restrict the interpretation. A
transcriptome-wide glutamate-codon-dose model is null, a later metastatic-state
dataset does not reproduce the original direction, and the current public data
do not distinguish mature-tRNA decoding from a tRNA-fragment route. We do not
claim that the two GAG codons are causal or that all three perturbations share
one mechanism.

**Required update before submission:** this paragraph must report completed
factorial tRNA/EIF4G2 leader experiments, uORF-start dependence, mature-tRNA
versus fragment discrimination, endogenous DAP5 output and the exact
peptide–HLA response. Without those experiments, this letter should not be sent
to *Nature*.

All primary sequencing data are public. We will provide a frozen code and
source-data archive that rebuilds every figure and retains the negative and
confounded contrasts. The manuscript is not under consideration elsewhere, and
all authors will have approved the final submission.
